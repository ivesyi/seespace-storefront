Shipping

This shop is operated from Shanghai, China. Orders are packed in 1–2 business days, then sent as a tracked international parcel.

We currently ship to the United States, the United Kingdom, Canada, and Australia. Import duties or taxes, if any, are set by the destination country and are the buyer’s responsibility.

Transit time varies by destination and carrier. Tracking is provided once the parcel is handed to the carrier.
